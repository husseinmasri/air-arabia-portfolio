01 · MASTER KEY VISUAL · THREE OPTIONS

One idea, three ways to fly.

Three expressions of the same idea: the Air Arabia plane as Christmas's new sleigh. Each one works as the master key visual; together they give the campaign range across formats and markets.

IDEA · CHRISTMAS TAKES FLIGHT · CONSTANT01 / 03
SCENE ▸ THE GIFTROLE · THE NEW SLEIGH
Why this direction

Every brand shows Christmas on the ground. We put it in the sky, where Air Arabia lives. The plane taking Santa's role gives the brand an instantly memorable part in the story, and its trail (a ribbon, a stream of gifts, a tree of light) turns one simple idea into three distinct pictures.

02 · INSIGHT, IDEA & AIR ARABIA'S ROLE

The best gifts can't be wrapped.

Audience insight

In Europe, Christmas is the most awaited moment of the year: lights, gifts, being together. Yet the gifts people remember are not the ones they unwrap. They are the moments they live: a reunion, a trip, a week of sun in the darkest month.

Creative idea

Christmas Takes Flight. Air Arabia becomes Christmas's new sleigh and delivers journeys, not just presents. The plane's trail becomes a ribbon, a stream of gifts, a tree of light. The gift is the journey.

Air Arabia's role

The airline that turns Christmas into a journey, connecting seven European markets to the sun, to family and to new places across the Middle East and beyond, at a price that fits the season.

Christmas Takes Flight.

One line, translated for each market, with the same promise everywhere: this Christmas, give a journey.

Clear roleSanta's sleigh is the most famous journey of Christmas. Air Arabia takes that role, so the brand is the hero of the story, not a logo at the end.
Born in the skyThe idea lives where the brand lives. Every scene is in the air or on the clouds.
Built to travelRibbon, tag and plane need no translation; only the words and the destinations change.
Made to engage"Who would you give a journey to?" is a question people want to answer, and tag someone in.
03 · ROUTES EXPLORED

Three routes, one decision.

Before landing on Christmas Takes Flight, three other routes were explored, each drawn in Air Arabia's own line-illustration style.

Why Christmas Takes Flight

It is the only route where Air Arabia is the hero of the story. The plane takes the sleigh's role, so the brand is not a sign-off but the reason Christmas happens. It works for every audience in every market, and it gives us a simple visual system (the trail, the ribbon, the tag) that adapts without losing the idea.

04 · WHAT STAYS, WHAT TRAVELS

One idea. Seven accents.

The brief asks what stays consistent across markets and what changes. The answer is a simple system: the picture and the promise stay, the words and the tag travel.

Consistent everywhere

  • The idea: the plane as Christmas's new sleigh.
  • The trail: red ribbon, stream of gifts or tree of light.
  • The lock-up: red logo tab, Dark Cherry headline box, Turquoise call to action.
  • The gift tag: the campaign's signature, with a red plane and a dotted flight path.
  • Typography & colour: Air Arabia typeface, Red Cherry, Dark Cherry, Turquoise.

Localised for each market

  • Language: the headline and copy run in each market's language (samples here are shown in English).
  • The local cue: one line on the Turquoise bar that speaks to the market's own Christmas.
  • The tag: the local city, the destination and the price in local currency.
  • The scene: a local landmark from the Across Europe visual in outdoor.
  • The moment: Sinterklaas, Wigilia, Christkind, Befana: each market's key date.
  • The media mix: weighted to how each country uses social and outdoor.

08 · DIGITAL ADVERTISING

A clear path to booking.

Message hierarchy

  1. Logo · the red Air Arabia tab, first and always visible.
  2. Headline · Christmas Takes Flight, in the market's language.
  3. Offer · destination and "from" price on the Turquoise tag.
  4. Call to action · "Book now" in white, the only button.

The objective is awareness with engagement, so ads run in two waves: early Advent drives to the social advent calendar ("Unwrap a journey"), and peak December switches to "Book now" with live fares.

10 · PRODUCTION APPROACH

One master, seven markets, every format.

Hero imagery

The three scenes are produced as CGI from Air Arabia's real A320 livery: the plane, the ribbon trail and the gift built in 3D, so every angle and format can be rendered at print quality. The concept images here were created with AI to set the direction.

A template system

One layered master file per scene. Only three layers change by market: the headline, the Turquoise cue and the gift tag. Seven markets across outdoor, social and digital come from the same masters, which keeps cost low and quality consistent.

Transcreation

Headlines and cues are written in English, then transcreated (not translated) by native copywriters in each market, and checked for tone and cultural fit before artwork.

Dynamic digital

Banners are built as dynamic creative: the city, destination and live fare on the gift tag are fed automatically by market and route, so every ad shows a real, bookable offer.

Social content

Advent Stories and the gift-tag GIF come from the template; TikTok content is made with local creators who untie the ribbon in their own way.

Timeline

  • Weeks 1–2: concept approval, CGI production.
  • Week 3: transcreation and market adaptation.
  • Week 4: quality check, media upload.
  • 24 Nov: tease goes live.

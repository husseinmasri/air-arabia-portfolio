04 · THE BRIEF, THE INSIGHT, THE IDEA

Nobody looks forward to flight time.

The brief

Build awareness of SkyTime, SkyCafé and 32-inch LegRoom, and position Air Arabia as an airline that offers an enjoyable travel experience, under one idea for a global audience.

Audience insight

Travellers see the flight as dead time: hours to get through before the trip really starts. Yet everyone knows that when you're enjoying yourself, you don't notice time passing.

Creative idea

Air Arabia doesn't make the flight shorter. It makes it feel shorter. SkyTime, SkyCafé and LegRoom are the reasons time flies on board.

Time flies.

Campaign line in English, with «الوقت يطير.» for Arabic-speaking markets.

Universal"Time flies" exists in almost every language, so it travels without translation.
Born from the brandSkyTime already carries the word "time". The idea grows out of the product.
Experience, not featuresWe show how people feel on board instead of listing what the airline offers.
Built to extendOne line, three endings: every product gets its own moment.
Why this direction

We chose an idea people already know is true. “Time flies” needs no explanation in any market, it grows out of the product name SkyTime, and it lets us talk about the experience rather than list features, which is exactly what the brief asks for.

07 · SOCIAL FILMS

"Already?"

One hero film and three product cutdowns. A montage of passengers on the same flight, each enjoying a different product, until the captain announces landing and someone looks up: "Already?".

Hero film · 30" · how to read it

One flight, many passengers. We follow a montage of travellers on the same flight, each lost in a different product, while a ticking clock speeds up into music. The captain announces landing, and a boy looks up: "Already?".

  • 0:00–0:04 Set-up: flight time 3h 10m.
  • 0:04–0:10 SkyTime: shows on their own devices.
  • 0:10–0:17 SkyCafé: a hot meal, lunch together.
  • 0:17–0:23 LegRoom: stretched out, at ease.
  • 0:23–0:30 Landing call, "Already?", end card.

Storyboard · hero film 30"

Why this direction

Everyone has heard a landing announcement. Ending on “Already?” turns that shared moment into proof of the idea. A montage of different passengers lets each product have its own moment while the story stays one flight, and the ticking clock that becomes music makes “time flies” something you can hear.

Sound & music direction

  • The tick becomes the track: a slow clock tick speeds up until it becomes the rhythm of an upbeat, percussive track.
  • Global with a regional accent: modern pop production with Arabic percussion.
  • Silence before the payoff: the music cuts on the landing call, so "Already?" lands in silence.
  • Sonic logo: the cabin chime "ding" closes every spot.

Moodboard & formats

  • Warm golden daylight; candid, unposed expressions; close framing on faces.
  • Red Cherry only inside the scene: headrests, cups, the SkyCafé box, crew uniform.
  • 16:9 (YouTube), 9:16 (Reels, TikTok), 1:1 (feed). The "Flight time" super is the two-second hook.

08 · OUTDOOR

Read in two seconds.

Every board carries one thought: a hero image, no more than seven words, and the logo.

The strongest location is the airport itself: exactly where travellers think about how long their flight will be.

Why this direction

Outdoor gets two seconds. We keep only the line, one emotional image and the logo. The wide 2:1 format holds the three products side by side, and the 1:1 series runs in sequence in airports, where travellers are already thinking about their flight.

09 · RADIO

You can hear time fly.

A ticking clock that speeds up as the passenger enjoys each product, until it becomes music, and stops on "Already?". Written as a script with sound cues, to be recorded with professional voice talent.

VO(warm, smiling) Three hours and ten minutes. Let's see…

SFXA long, relaxed stretch and sigh. The beat becomes an upbeat track.

PASSENGER(genuinely surprised) Already?
VOAir Arabia. Time flies.
SFXCabin chime: ding.
Why this direction

Radio has no pictures, so the idea has to live in sound. A clock that speeds up as the passenger enjoys each product makes “time flies” audible, and the silence before “Already?” gives the line its punch. The cabin chime links radio to the films as one sonic signature.

Sound cues

  • Clock tick: the campaign's audio device, from slow to fast to music.
  • Product sounds: screen tap and laughter (SkyTime), trolley and box (SkyCafé), stretch and sigh (LegRoom).
  • Silence before "Already?" so the payoff lands; the cabin chime as the sonic logo.

Arabic version

Recorded with an Arabic voiceover for Arabic-speaking markets, same sound design. Tagline: «العربية للطيران. الوقت يطير.»

12 · PRODUCTION, ROLLOUT & MEASUREMENT

From concept to campaign.

One shoot, every asset

A two-day shoot in a real Air Arabia A320 cabin (or a matching set) with casting from the GCC, the India region and Eastern Europe. One shoot delivers key visuals, product stills, the hero film, cutdowns, bumpers and social content.

Rollout

  • Launch: key visual, outdoor, hero film, radio.
  • Sustain: product films, carousel, Stories, product outdoor.
  • Always on: route series, comment replies, GIFs.

How we'd measure it

  • Awareness of SkyTime, SkyCafé and LegRoom (brand lift).
  • Social engagement: swipe-through rate, saves, route comments.
  • Film completion rate and ad recall.
  • Trade: partner asset downloads.
